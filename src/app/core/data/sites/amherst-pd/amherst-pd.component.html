HOMETOWN Lynchburg, Virginia DATE REPORTED MISSING October 18, 2024

SUMMARY

Emily Carter was reported missing on October 18, 2024 after failing to return home following an evening appointment in downtown Amherst.

Investigators determined that Carter was last seen during the evening of October 17, 2024. Her vehicle was located the following morning.

WITNESS STATEMENT

A local business patron reported seeing Carter shortly after 10:00 PM near the municipal parking area.
